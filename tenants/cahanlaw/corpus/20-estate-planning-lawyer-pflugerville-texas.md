Estate planning law is the area of the law that regulates managing your assets, property, and affairs during your lifetime and after your death. It generally involves creating legal documents such as wills, trusts, powers of attorney, and healthcare directives. It usually involves strategies for minimizing taxes and protecting assets from creditors and legal disputes as well.

Navigating this area of the law does not require a lawyer in a strictly legal sense. However, seeking a lawyer for preparing estate planning documents will pay dividends in maximizing having your wealth dispersed as you want. On top of this, a lack of experience in estate planning law will generally mean you will not be successful in your endeavors on your own.
