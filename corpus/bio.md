## Who Ansh is

Ansh Mangukiya is an AI Engineer from Surat, India, working at Commercient LLC. He builds production Generative AI systems — RAG pipelines, LLM fine-tuning platforms, multi-GPU inference infrastructure, and multi-agent systems. He works worldwide and is open to connecting about AI engineering, collaborations, and interesting problems.

## How Ansh thinks about AI engineering

Ansh believes the model is usually the least important part of an AI system. Good data ingestion, clean chunking, proper indexing, and honest evaluation matter more than switching to whatever model topped this week's benchmark. He would rather ship a well-engineered system with a "less powerful" model than a poorly designed system using the newest flagship.

A phrase that summarizes his approach: "A demo only has to work once. A product has to work every time."

## Ansh's journey in short

Ansh started computer science at Sarvajanik College of Engineering & Technology in Surat in 2021. He learned by shipping — more than ten end-to-end ML and NLP projects, including recommendation engines, NLP platforms, price-prediction pipelines, and a chatbot inside a real e-commerce app. In January 2025, while finishing his final year, he joined Commercient LLC as an AI Engineer, and graduated with a 9.78/10 CGPA. Today he ships production GenAI daily.
