## Our Philosophy

We believe the best legal service is built on trust, empathy and respect. Our clients deserve respect, skilled advocacy and exceptional service.

If you understand the true dynamics of the Texas real estate business, you realize that:

- Good landlords need good tenants, or they wouldn’t have a viable business or real profitable estate investment.
- Good tenants deserve good landlords including owner’s authorized agent, manager or management company.

Landlords expect their tenants – to take good care of the landlord’s property, not disrupt neighbors, not violate their lease agreement and live their lives in peace and enjoyment.

Tenants expect their landlord – to provide clean, safe, habitable, functional properties free of defects, vermin, pests, rodents, mold, health and safety issues; with quick to respond to any maintenance issues that might arise so the tenant(s) can enjoy a peaceful home for their themselves and their loved ones.

Unfortunately, disputes arise between landlords and tenants. Misunderstandings can spiral ‘out of control’ making for an untenable situation to where reaching out to a third party — a skilled Real Estate & Eviction Lawyer — for practical and legal advice, to help negotiate a truce or settlement over a specific issue, or legal representation, is the smart and savvy decision. Attorney Richard Cahan is here to help where he is able.
