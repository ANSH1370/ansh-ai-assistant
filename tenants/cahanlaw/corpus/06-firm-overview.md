## Probate in Texas

Probate in Texas is more streamlined than other states but can still be overwhelming. Attorney Cahan helps clients determine (1) is probate is necessary, and if so (2) which probate jurisdiction to file, (3) which of the three forms of Texas probate to choose, and then follows through as his clients instruct. Understandably, many clients are emotionally overwhelmed and ask Richard to handle everything including filing the probate, administering the probate process, and make any necessary court appearances. Other clients retain Richard’s probate services on an “as-needed basis” where they do some of the probate tasks and have Richard do the forms, form filing, answer legal questions as they come up, and make any court appearances on their behalf.

No matter the level of involvement required in your probate matter, Austin probate attorney, Richard Cahan, strives to ensure that his clients’ wishes regarding disposition of estate assets is accomplished in an efficient and cost-effective manner.
