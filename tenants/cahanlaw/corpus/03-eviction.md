## Secure Your Property Against Bad Tenants

If you’re a landlord struggling with a tenant who has stopped paying rent or violated the lease agreement, you need an experienced eviction lawyer to ensure your rights are protected. Texas Property Code requires landlords to provide a three-day notice to vacate before filing an eviction lawsuit. Any violations of these requirements—such as improperly serving the eviction notice or attempting to remove the tenant’s belongings—can be used as a defense by the tenant, delaying the eviction process and potentially costing you more in lost rent.
